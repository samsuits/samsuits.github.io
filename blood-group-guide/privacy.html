Blood Group Guide

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Blood Group Guide does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

  • Details you enter on the Me page (name, blood group, sex, last donation date, notes) are saved only on your device.
  • The Android app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • There are no ads, analytics, trackers, or accounts.
  • You can remove your details any time with “Delete my details”, or by uninstalling the app / clearing this page’s site data.
